## English {#en}

**In short:** Routine Champs has no internet access. It collects no personal data, shows no
ads and contains no analytics or tracking. What you enter stays on your device.

### What the app keeps, and where

To work, Routine Champs keeps the following on your device:

- your children's first names, and the avatars and colours chosen for them;
- task lists, tasks, times and days of the week;
- coin balances and the price of a reward;
- the parent PIN and settings such as the language and the start notification;
- the date the free trial started, and whether the unlock has been bought.

This stays on your device. Routine Champs does not send it to us or to anyone else, and we
cannot see it.

### Spoken reminders

Routine Champs reads reminders aloud, for example a child's name and their next task. It does
this through the text-to-speech engine selected in your device's Android settings (often
Google's Speech Services). That engine is a separate app, provided under its own privacy
policy. Most engines speak on the device itself; some can use an internet connection for
certain voices. You can choose or change the engine in Android's settings, under
*Text-to-speech output*.

### Android backup

Android may include the app's data in the device backup to your Google account, and restore
it when you set up a new device. That backup is made and kept by Google, under Google's
privacy policy, not by us. How you manage or delete it depends on your device's backup
settings.

### Purchases

After a free trial of 7 days, Routine Champs can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.
Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's privacy policy. The app only asks
Google Play whether the unlock has been bought. We never receive your payment details. Google
Play gives every developer order details for accounting and refunds (such as the order
number, product, price and country); we use them for nothing else.

### Permissions

- **Notifications** – an optional notification when a task list starts. Only if you turn it
  on.
- **Run at startup** and **prevent the device from sleeping** – to keep that notification
  scheduled after the device restarts.
- **Vibration** – a short vibration when a task is finished.
- **Google Play billing** – to buy and restore the unlock.
- **View network connections** – requested by the Google Play Billing Library. Routine Champs
  itself has no internet permission and cannot connect to the internet.

### Children

Routine Champs is made to be used by children together with a parent. It collects no personal
information from anyone, children included. Setting up the app, the purchase, and the link to
this policy are behind a parent PIN.

### Deleting your data

Uninstalling Routine Champs, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes its data
from the device. A backup Android made earlier may remain in your Google account and be
restored later, until you remove it there.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
